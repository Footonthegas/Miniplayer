Premium activation checklist

  • Confirm the Razorpay checkout is completed.
  • Allow time for the subscription webhook to reach the Premium server.
  • Open the extension welcome page or popup and choose “I have paid — check Premium”.
  • If the server is asleep on a free hosting plan, the first request may take longer. If activation still fails, contact support with the subscription reference.
